The Reading Den

A two-screen reading session for one grown-up and one child, matched to the Little Wandle phonics progression.

🧑‍🏫 Reader portal Your screen. Pick the level, run the activities, keep the record. Start here. 🧒 His screen Just the story or game — no menus, nothing to wander off into.

Seven activities

Flashcards, Stories, Word Sort, 3 in a Row, Mystery Word, Word Wheel and a Whiteboard — the same shape of session a school reading volunteer runs.

Eleven books

Proper little stories with covers, characters and an ending — every word checked so it only uses sounds he's actually been taught.

Two screens, live

Run the portal on your laptop and his screen on a tablet. Click a word and it highlights on his page.
