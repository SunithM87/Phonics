🦉 The Reading Den
Little Wandle-aligned · Phases 2–5

A home reading corner, just for you two.

A grown-up "coach" screen for picking sounds, words, and stories at the right level, and a simple, distraction-free screen for him to read from — the same split-screen idea as a school reading platform, built from scratch for home, and matched to the phonics scheme his school uses.

🧑‍🏫 I'm the grown-up (Coach view) 🧒 Go straight to Kid view

How it works

Open Coach view first. Tick off which sounds and tricky words his school has already taught, choose today's activity, then hit Start to switch this screen into Kid view — or open Kid view on a second device/tab and it'll follow along automatically.

Five activities, three games

Sound Cards · Build & Blend · Tricky Words · Story Time · Alien Word Check for structured practice, plus Three in a Row, Match Pairs, and Word Bingo for when reading should feel like playing.

Synced across every screen in the house

Running this through the included sync server (see the README — it's the setup for hosting it on a NAS) means Coach and Kid views stay live-synced across any devices on the network, not just two tabs of one browser. Look for the 🟢/🟡 pill on either screen to see whether that's active.
